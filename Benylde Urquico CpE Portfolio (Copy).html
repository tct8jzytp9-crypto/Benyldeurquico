LearnSmart

Adaptive learning site paired with a low-cost, microcontroller-based quiz device for accessible learning in public schools (SDG 4: Quality Education).

Key features: teacher creates a quiz and gets a unique code; students enter the code on the device keypad; the device fetches the quiz over Wi-Fi; teacher dashboard shows answers and who needs help; rechargeable battery.
